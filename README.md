### About the Experiment

This experiment demonstrates the separation of a complex biological sample on SDS-PAGE and the detection of a specific cytosolic protein from a crude mixture using the immunoblotting (Western blotting) technique.
Proteins resolved according to their molecular weight are electro-transferred onto a nitrocellulose or PVDF membrane, which is then blocked to prevent non-specific antibody binding.
The membrane is probed with a primary antibody against the protein of interest, followed by an enzyme-conjugated secondary antibody.
The blot is finally developed by colorimetric, chemiluminescent or fluorescent detection and imaged using a gel-doc system.
